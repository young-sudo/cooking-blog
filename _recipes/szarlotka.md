---
title: Szarlotka
category: sweet
date: 2026-11-18
time: 1 h 15 min
servings: 8
---
## Ingredients
- 300 g flour, 200 g cold butter, 100 g sugar, 2 yolks
- 1.5 kg tart apples
- 1 tsp cinnamon

## Steps
1. Rub butter into flour and sugar, add yolks, form a dough and chill.
2. Grate the apples, cook briefly with cinnamon, drain.
3. Press two thirds of the dough into a tin, add apples, grate the rest on top.
4. Bake 50 minutes at 180 °C.
