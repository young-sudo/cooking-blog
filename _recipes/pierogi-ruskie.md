---
title: Pierogi ruskie
category: savory
date: 2026-11-15
time: 1 h 30 min
servings: 4
---
## Ingredients
- 500 g flour, 250 ml warm water, pinch of salt
- 500 g potatoes, boiled and mashed
- 250 g farmer's cheese
- 1 onion, fried

## Steps
1. Knead the dough and rest it for 30 minutes.
2. Mix potatoes, cheese and half the onion; season well.
3. Roll thin, cut circles, fill and pinch shut.
4. Boil until they float plus 2 minutes; top with the remaining onion.
