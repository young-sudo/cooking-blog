## Ingredients
- 500 ml sour rye starter
- 1 l stock
- 2 white sausages
- 2 potatoes, cubed
- 2 cloves garlic, marjoram
- 2 hard-boiled eggs

## Steps
1. Simmer the sausages in stock for 20 minutes, then slice.
2. Cook the potatoes in the stock until tender.
3. Stir in the starter, garlic and marjoram; heat without boiling.
4. Serve with sausage and halved eggs.
