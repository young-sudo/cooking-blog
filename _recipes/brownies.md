## Ingredients
- 200 g dark chocolate
- 150 g butter
- 200 g sugar
- 3 eggs
- 80 g flour, pinch of salt

## Steps
1. Melt chocolate and butter together.
2. Whisk in sugar, then eggs one at a time.
3. Fold in flour and salt.
4. Bake 25 minutes at 180 °C; the centre should stay soft.
