title: Shakshuka
category: savory
date: 2026-11-20
time: 30 min
servings: 2
---
## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- Salt, pepper, parsley

## Steps
1. Soften the onion and pepper in oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices, cook 1 minute.
3. Pour in tomatoes, simmer 10 minutes until thick.
4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–7 minutes.
5. Season and finish with parsley.
